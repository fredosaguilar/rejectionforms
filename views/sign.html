Consent to use electronic records and signatures

What you are agreeing to

You are being asked to review and sign this document electronically. If you agree, your electronic signature will have the same legal effect as a handwritten one under the federal ESIGN Act (15 U.S.C. ch. 96) and the Washington Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (RCW 19.360).

Getting a paper copy

You may request a paper copy of this document at no charge by contacting Columbia Basin Insurance. Requesting a paper copy does not mean you have withdrawn your consent.

Withdrawing consent

You may decline to sign electronically at any time before you sign, using the Decline button below. To withdraw consent after signing, or to ask for records on paper instead, contact Columbia Basin Insurance. Withdrawing consent does not affect the validity of records you have already signed electronically.

What you need to view and keep these records

  • A current web browser with cookies and JavaScript enabled
  • A working email address — the one this request was sent to
  • A PDF reader, and the ability to download or print to keep a copy

Keeping your copy

Once all parties have signed, a completed copy including a certificate of completion will be emailed to you. You may download and retain it at any time.

Keeping your contact details current

Tell us if your email address changes, so that records can continue to reach you.
